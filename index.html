About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Southeast University. My research focuses on kinetic methods, phase-field modeling, multiphase flows, and gas-solid interactions.

Research Interests

  • Discrete kinetic methods
  • DUGKS / DKS
  • Phase-field and diffuse-interface methods
  • Rarefied gas-solid interactions
  • Multiphase and phase-change flows
